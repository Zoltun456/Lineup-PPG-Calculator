How PPG works

A player uses their position in your rankings. Rank mode uses the position and rank you enter. PPG follows the scoring format selected in Settings, and ranks beyond the dataset use its last available value.

How VOR works

Value over replacement subtracts the PPG at your calculated league-wide replacement rank from the selected rank’s PPG.

Reproducible local snapshot

scripts/collect-data.mjs downloads completed-season statistics and the current NFL roster from nflverse, then joins roster-eligible players to Sleeper fantasy metadata. Retired and stale teamless records are excluded before the local files are generated. The browser never contacts either provider directly.
